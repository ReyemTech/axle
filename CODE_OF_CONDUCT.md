# Code of conduct

Axle follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces is expected to follow it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **contact@reyem.tech**. Reports are
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainers apply the enforcement
guidelines described in the Covenant.
